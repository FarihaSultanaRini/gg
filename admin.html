Admin workspace guide

How admin manages
all the GG apps

Staff signs into one low-code workspace. It uses the GG backend to view customer, driver and workshop records and carry out permitted actions.

Admin workspace → secured staff API → GG backend → updated app records

How admin gets access

Admin uses their own staff account. Access to a record or action depends on their permissions.

Pink: staff workspace · Yellow: permission check · Blue: backend · Green: allowed result

1. Staff signs in with MFA

The identity provider verifies the named account and issues a short-lived staff session. Expired or revoked sessions must sign in again.

↓
2. Backend reads the assigned staff role

The account already has its approved permissions. Staff does not choose a more powerful role at sign-in.

Proposed role split: client confirmation still needed↓

Ops operator

Day-to-day operations

Can view

  • Partner applications and permitted evidence.
  • Allowed orders, matching/count/route cases, support/privacy cases and essential reports.

Can change

  • Create, activate or suspend approved partner accounts; grant approved Driver/Workshop modules.
  • Resolve allowed matching/count/support/privacy cases and use permitted pre-departure route controls.

Only within assigned city and case permissions. Finance/configuration commands need the extra role.

Ops admin

Ops operator permissions, plus extra controls

Can view

  • The same allowed operations records.
  • Authorized payment/earnings records, configuration, exports and audit history.

Can change

  • Prices, zones, hours and other approved configuration.
  • Approved payment/refund, payout and subscription-correction actions; request permitted CSV exports.
  • Emergency route changes and break-glass access only with the designated permission.

Every action still needs record access and state checks. Original audit, custody and money history stays unchanged.

One Partner app — two modules

Driver and Workshop are inside the same app. A partner may be approved for one module or both. Admin access below depends on the assigned staff role, city and case permissions.

Driver module

Driver sees/does: follow the assigned route and stops; record pickup, QR handoffs and delivery proof.

Admin can view

Allowed driver account/approval records, assigned routes/stops and reported incidents.

Admin can do

Review, create, activate or suspend approved accounts and grant the approved Driver module. Manage permitted route exceptions.

After departure, emergency stop/reassignment needs the designated Ops admin permission.

Workshop module

Workshop sees/does: handle assigned orders; record intake counts, processing, quality checks and return readiness.

Admin can view

Allowed workshop account/approval records, assigned orders, original counts and permitted custody/case evidence.

Admin can do

Review, create, activate or suspend approved accounts and grant the approved Workshop module. Resolve allowed matching/count cases with reasons and evidence.

Keep original counts. Processing still needs a resolved mismatch and the required custody scan.

Partner access step
  1. When approving the account, staff grants Driver, Workshop or both approved modules.
  2. The partner signs into the Partner app.
  3. The backend returns the approved modules through GET /v1/partner/modules.
  4. The app shows only those modules. The backend also checks each module's requests.

PAR-001/002/004. Workshop uses the documented /v1/provider/** API paths.

Before showing data or saving a change, the backend checks the staff member’s permissions and assigned city. Staff can open only allowed records and details, and use only actions allowed for that record right now. Changes are saved in the backend; each app then loads the updated information its user may see. Refs: OPS-001/011/012; API-002/003/010; SEC-003/004.

Customer management

Find the order and open the case

Staff searches within their granted city and opens an authorized order or support case. List views mask personal details; sensitive evidence needs case-level access.

  • Review order snapshots, custody and available evidence.
  • Record and communicate the support/claim outcome.
  • Handle authorized privacy requests.
  • Payment and entitlement corrections require extra permissions.

Driver management

Review accounts and assigned work

Partners submit compliance evidence through the approved secure form. Staff reviews it before creating and activating the driver account.

  • Review documents/training and approved module grants.
  • Create, activate, correct, suspend or deactivate accounts with a reason.
  • Inspect routes, assignment failures and driver incidents.
  • Before departure: allowed dispatch controls. After departure: audited emergency stop/reassignment only.

Send the allowed staff action

The workspace calls the partner or routing staff APIs. The Driver module then reads its approved account access and current assignment from the backend.

Workshop management

Review accounts and exceptions

Staff activates workshops after secure-form review. The Workshop module reads only its assigned orders and operational details.

  • Review compliance evidence and activate/suspend the workshop account.
  • Inspect matching exceptions after the candidate pool is exhausted.
  • Review garment-count mismatches and submit an audited resolution.
  • Preserve both original counts and the customer impact record.

“Admin” does not mean every staff member can do everything

The access-control document proposes two staff roles. Their grouping still needs client confirmation; the backend enforces each permission.

Proposed staff rolePermitted areaApp connection
ops-operatorPartner review, matching/count/route/support/privacy cases, scoped search and essential metrics.Manages authorized customer cases and partner work through staff APIs.
ops-adminAdds configuration, payment/payout commands, entitlement correction, CSV export, audit search and designated break-glass access.Approved changes affect the same backend records the apps read. Emergency routing needs its designated permission.

Every request also checks city, record, fields and current action/state. A hidden button alone is not access control. Source: docs/architecture/access-control.md, sections 2–6.

Keep staff identity on every action. A workspace API client credential identifies the integration. Each action must also carry the authenticated operator’s identity, scopes and revocable session. It does not grant shared staff authority.
