Database design guide

How the backend records fit together

The Customer app, Partner app and staff workspace use the same backend. The backend checks each request and owns the records in one logical PostgreSQL database.

Planned logical design. The names below come from docs/architecture/data-model.md. They describe records and their responsibilities. Final table names, columns, foreign keys, the physical schema, Prisma migrations and the data dictionary are separate deliverables.

How all three clients reach the records

Customer appOwn profile, booking and order
Partner appApproved Driver / Workshop modules
Staff workspaceAllowed cases and staff actions
↓
Secured APIs + NestJS backendAuthenticate, authorize, validate state/evidence and handle safe retries
↓
One logical PostgreSQL databaseEach domain owns its records. Prisma migrations own schema changes.

Photos and documents live in private object storage. The database keeps their references and access metadata; the backend provides permitted, time-limited access.

Order status and payment status are separate

The order status describes the laundry journey. Payment status describes money. A failed charge must remain visible as a payment exception; it must not invent a successful payment or erase custody facts. Processing/delivery hold policy is still pending under D-013.

Rules the database and backend must enforce

Keep the original order facts

Changing a customer address or a current price must not rewrite a placed order. Read its saved snapshots for historical calculations.

Spec: DAT-003; PAY-005; CFG-003
MD: data-model.md sections 5 E-1, 7; access-control.md sections 4, 7

Do not assign twice

Two simultaneous acceptances must not create two active workshops for an order or two owners for one route. Assignment and capacity checks commit together.

Spec: DAT-005; MAT-003; MAT-007; MAT-008
MD: data-model.md sections 3.6, 5 E-3/E-7; access-control.md sections 4, 7

Do not charge twice

Record the final calculation before calling the gateway. Use the same business-operation key for retries. A timeout is an uncertain result to reconcile, not proof that a second charge is safe.

Spec: PAY-003; PAY-007; PAY-008; API-003
MD: data-model.md sections 3.9, 5 E-4; access-control.md sections 4, 7

A mismatch keeps both counts

Save the driver count and workshop count. Staff adds the confirmed count, reason, evidence, customer impact, identity and time; processing also needs the workshop custody scan.

Spec: OPS-006; SPV-005; ORD-005; PAY-010
MD: data-model.md sections 3.8, 4.2, 7; access-control.md sections 4, 7

Preserve history

Audit, ledger and custody records are append-only for application actors. Corrections add linked records. Lawful privacy processing uses the separately controlled retention mechanism; it must preserve financial amounts and event facts.

Spec: DAT-007; ORD-010; FIN-003; PRV-007; PRV-008
MD: data-model.md sections 5 E-2, 8; access-control.md sections 4, 7

Use safe units

Store money in integer minor units with a currency code and timestamps in UTC. Use one precision-safe weight unit; integer grams is the proposed baseline pending design review.

Spec: DAT-001; DAT-002; PAY-006
MD: data-model.md sections 2, 5 E-5; access-control.md sections 4, 7

Protect access

Apps and the low-code workspace request data through APIs. Backend checks role, city, record, fields and action. An order ID or QR alone never grants permission.

Spec: OPS-012; API-010; API-002; SEC-004
MD: data-model.md sections 2, 3.1, 6; access-control.md sections 4, 7

What still needs to be built

  1. Physical schema and data dictionary: choose exact tables, columns, types, relationships and constraints that satisfy this logical design.
  2. Prisma migrations: implement one controlled schema-change path, including any required raw-SQL indexes or geospatial support.
  3. Seed and configuration instructions: use approved configuration. Required pending decisions must be resolved for launch.
  4. Verification: check fresh installation, upgrades, concurrent assignments, safe retries, one final charge, immutable history, permissions and lawful retention behavior.

City/time zone and residency remain D-001; identity D-004; payment gateway D-005; capacity D-008; retention D-019. The source design also flags draft cleanup, weight-unit review and staff recovery transitions for confirmation.

API-002
APIs SHALL use authenticated subject/role context and object-level authorization; resource identifiers alone SHALL never grant access.
API-003
Create, payment, assignment, scan, state, and staff mutation endpoints SHALL accept idempotency keys and return a stable outcome for safe retries.
API-006
External callbacks SHALL require signature verification or equivalent authentication, replay protection, timestamp tolerance, deduplication, and asynchronous processing.
API-010
No mobile or low-code client SHALL contain privileged service credentials or connect directly to the primary database.
ARC-001
Domain modules SHALL own their rules and data access boundaries even if deployed together; cross-module changes SHALL use explicit service interfaces and transactions/events.
ARC-005
Background processing SHALL support retry with backoff, dead-letter/exception handling, idempotency, correlation IDs, and health metrics.
CFG-003
Orders, routes, assignments, and ledger calculations SHALL retain the configuration version used so historical behavior is reproducible.
CLM-001
A customer SHALL be able to submit a simple Report an issue form against a delivered order with issue category, description, and optional evidence. The form SHALL create a staff case; it need not implement a customer-facing claim workflow.
CLM-004
Staff SHALL record the claim decision in the low-code workspace, including approved amount by garment, reason, explanation, decision-maker, timestamps, and payout/refund reference.
COC-003
The system SHALL maintain one canonical bag-label identity per order and a separate print history with printer/device, actor, reason, and timestamp.
COC-005
Each custody event SHALL include from/to custodian, route/stop when applicable, scan result, UTC server time, device-reported time, and location when permission is available.
COC-007
Garment catalog images SHALL use private object storage and time-limited access; delivery proof images SHALL be visible only to the relevant customer and authorized staff.
CUS-002
The system SHALL verify the phone/email as selected in discovery and SHALL validate/geocode the home address before first order.
DAT-001
All operational timestamps SHALL be stored in UTC with source/device context where relevant and rendered in the configured city time zone.
DAT-002
All amounts SHALL use integer minor units and currency code; all billable/capacity weights SHALL use one canonical precision-safe internal unit with explicit conversions.
DAT-003
Every order SHALL retain snapshots of address, service, prices, tax, payout inputs, windows, terms/consents, and relevant configuration versions.
DAT-005
Relational constraints and transactional locks SHALL enforce one active provider assignment, one active route owner, valid capacity reservation, and unique idempotent financial/custody events.
DAT-007
Audit, ledger, and custody history SHALL be append-only for application actors; corrections SHALL reference superseded values through new events.
DAT-008
The vendor SHALL deliver a logical data model, physical schema/migrations, data dictionary, retention matrix, and seed/configuration instructions.
DRV-005
The pilot route SHALL lock when the driver starts it. Staff MAY apply an audited emergency override; the solver SHALL NOT automatically insert new stops after departure.
DRV-006
At customer pickup, the driver SHALL open the unattended bag as authorized, count garments, record pickup weight, reconcile Full Service ironing photos, create the digital manifest, and report prohibited/unsafe items.
DRV-007
The driver's recorded pickup weight SHALL be the authoritative billing weight. Any later provider weight variance SHALL be logged without changing the customer charge.
FIN-003
Partner financial records SHALL use an append-only subledger; corrections SHALL use adjustment entries with reason and authorized actor.
MAT-003
The system SHALL send an exclusive, expiring provider offer to one candidate at a time and atomically reserve capacity only upon valid acceptance.
MAT-007
Matching commands and offer responses SHALL use transactional concurrency control so late responses cannot overwrite a newer assignment.
MAT-008
Capacity reservations SHALL be released on decline, timeout, cancellation, deactivation, or approved override and SHALL be reconciled by a scheduled integrity job.
MSG-001
The pilot SHALL support in-app notification inbox and push notifications. SMS and transactional email are outside scope.

Authorized staff SHALL configure customer prices, minimum charges, delivery fees, subscription price/benefit, provider payout rates, and driver route earning components without a software deployment.
OPS-004
Staff SHALL review invited driver/provider submissions collected through an approved secure form, including evidence, training status, and compliance checklist; create and activate approved accounts or record correction, rejection, suspension, or deactivation with a reason.
OPS-006
Staff SHALL resolve a count discrepancy by recording the confirmed count, reason, evidence references, customer impact, resolver, and timestamp. Original driver and provider values SHALL remain immutable.
OPS-012
The low-code tool SHALL consume documented APIs and event feeds; direct database connections and embedded production credentials are prohibited.
ORD-001
Every state-changing command SHALL be idempotent and reject stale or unauthorized transitions without losing the attempted action from security/diagnostic logs.
ORD-002
Every accepted transition SHALL append an immutable event containing order, event type, prior/new state, actor, role, UTC timestamp, correlation ID, and relevant evidence references.
ORD-005
An order SHALL NOT enter PROCESSING while a count discrepancy is unresolved or required provider custody scan is absent.
ORD-006
An order SHALL NOT enter READY_FOR_RETURN until service completion, quality check, clean-bag repack, and same-QR reprint are recorded.
ORD-007
An order SHALL NOT enter DELIVERED until the required photo, GPS, timestamp, and route-stop evidence are durably stored.
ORD-008
Financial sub-statuses SHALL track payment-method validation, final charge, settlement, refund/correction, and exception independently from physical order state.
ORD-010
Staff correction SHALL create a compensating event; production history SHALL never be rewritten to conceal an earlier value.
PAR-001
The Partner app SHALL display only the module(s) for which the signed-in partner has been manually approved.
PAR-002
Partner applications and documents SHALL be collected outside the app through an approved secure form. After staff review, approved accounts SHALL be created and activated by staff; the app SHALL support first sign-in and suspended/inactive states only.
PAY-003
Successful booking SHALL validate and save the payment method without charging it. After the driver completes pickup weight and count, the system SHALL make the final charge exactly once using an order-specific idempotency key.
PAY-005
Every order SHALL store immutable snapshots of currency, tax-inclusive display amount, tax amount/rule version, price components, rounding method, and customer-visible explanation.
PAY-006
All money SHALL be stored as integer minor units with explicit ISO currency code. Floating-point arithmetic is prohibited for financial calculations.
PAY-007
The final charge calculation SHALL be deterministic, replayable from snapshots, and posted to an append-only customer ledger before invoking the gateway.
PAY-008
Gateway webhooks SHALL be signature-verified, deduplicated, processed asynchronously, and reconciled against internal payment intent/status; webhook order SHALL NOT be assumed.
PAY-010
Staff-initiated refunds/corrections, reversals, disputes, goodwill credits, and write-offs SHALL retain actor/reason, source order, gateway reference, and ledger entries; no automatic second charge follows a provider count discrepancy.
PRV-007
Users SHALL be able to initiate access, correction, and account-deletion requests in-app. Deletion SHALL remove or de-identify data not subject to a documented legal/security retention exception.
PRV-008
The product SHALL explain what cannot be immediately deleted, why, and the retention period; backups SHALL follow a documented expiry process rather than ad hoc deletion.
RTE-003
The solver SHALL support pickup-and-delivery precedence, four-hour time windows, driver/vehicle capacity, driver availability, route start/end policy, service time, travel time, and provider readiness/receiving hours.
RTE-007
Every saved plan SHALL be versioned with solver/input versions, cutoff, jobs, constraints, objective result, runtime, and unassigned reasons.
RTE-009
A route SHALL lock at driver start. The pilot SHALL NOT automatically insert or reorder stops after departure; this is the first deferred feature if scope pressure occurs.
SEC-003
Staff SHALL use multi-factor authentication. Customer/partner authentication and recovery methods are TBD but SHALL include verified contact, brute-force protection, secure session rotation, revocation, and suspicious-login handling.
SEC-004
Authorization SHALL be deny-by-default and enforced at API, object, field, action, and city/assignment scope. The vendor SHALL test cross-role and cross-order access systematically.
SEC-007
Uploads SHALL enforce authenticated purpose, type/size limits, malware/content checks appropriate to risk, metadata handling, private storage, and time-limited download authorization.
SPV-005
A count mismatch SHALL pause the order in COUNT_DISCREPANCY. Processing SHALL remain blocked until the count is reconciled or staff records an audited resolution.
SUB-004
The Garments Grid entitlement SHALL have only active and inactive states, with gateway subscription reference, effective timestamp, and last verified timestamp. Detailed billing lifecycle states remain in the payment gateway.
SUB-007
The order's delivery-fee decision SHALL remain historically reproducible even if the subscription later expires or rules change.
